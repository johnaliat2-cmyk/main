## Brand & Style

The design system projects an authoritative, visionary, and academically grounded institutional ethos tailored for high-level educational technology, impact ventures, and investment governance. It communicates institutional gravitas, pedagogical insight, and commercial viability. The visual language blends traditional British collegiate rigor with agile financial technology polish.

### Visual Principles
- **Scholarly Authority with Strategic Momentum**: Clean structures, rigorous information architecture, and metric-forward clarity. Content should feel thoroughly researched, validated, and executive-ready.
- **Editorial Contrast**: Deep academic navies paired with crisp whites and light architectural slate surfaces, energized by precise highlights of institutional gold and deliberate crimson interventions.
- **Precision Structuring**: Generous breathing space combined with fine hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`), high-contrast structural columns, and metric displays that respect rigorous compliance and institutional due diligence.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast traditional tones reinterpreted with modern digital precision.

### Functional Palette Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#1D3A50`)**: Represents institutional stability. Used for primary typography, dominant header containers, hero blocks, dark structural bands, and primary iconography.
- **Deep Navy (`#152C3D`)**: The anchor tone. Reserved for the sub-footer, ultra-deep high-contrast surfaces, and contextual hover states on navy backgrounds.
- **Crimson (`#D11F4C`)**: Direct action and narrative checkpoints. Applied strictly to primary interactive targets (CTA buttons), timeline indices, active indicators, and the prominent 3px global rule directly under the primary navigation bar.
- **Crimson Dark (`#B01840`)**: State change tone. Used exclusively for hovered/active primary CTA buttons.
- **Academic Gold (`#F8C945`)**: Intellectual illumination. Applied to editorial metadata labels (e.g., `01 / THE CLASSROOM NEED`), vertical hero anchor lines, table header underlines, and prominent KPI highlights.
- **Panel / Surface (`#F2F4F8`)**: The contextual canvas. Softens reading fatigue across alternating content modules, modular card backdrops, data rows, and structured metadata callouts.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Base canvas for primary narrative zones, crisp raised card surfaces, and input controls.
- **Border / Hairline (`#E2E8F0`)**: Low-contrast boundaries for data tables, card perimeters, and modular separators.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy centers on **Lato**, balancing humanist proportions with structural geometry. 

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Body Text**: Standard long-form reading text defaults to 17px (`body-md`) with a generous 28px line height to ensure clarity across dense pedagogical analysis and prospectus reports.
- **Section Labels**: Editorial sub-headers (e.g., `01 / THE CLASSROOM NEED`) are uppercase, rendered in `section-label` (13px, 700 weight, +0.14em letter-spacing). On dark `#1D3A50` backgrounds, they must be displayed in Academic Gold (`#F8C945`); on light surfaces, use Navy (`#1D3A50`) or Crimson (`#D11F4C`).
- **Headlines**: Weighted at 700 to 900 in Navy (`#1D3A50`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Avoid thin weights for titles to maintain an authoritative tone.
- **Disclaimers & Footnotes**: Compliance notes, regulatory frameworks, and investor warnings use `caption` (12px) in muted slate `#64748B`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column institutional grid designed for structured data presentations, split narrative sections, and asymmetric executive callouts.

### Responsive Grids & Canvas
- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12-column grid with a max container width of 1280px, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, and minimum 48px (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column fluid grid, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Alternating Bands
Full-width band transitions demarcate thought leadership phases:
- Alternate between Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Panel / Surface (`#F2F4F8`) for standard sections.
- Anchor sections (such as Hero headers, investment executive summaries, and final call-to-actions) utilize dark Navy (`#1D3A50`) bands.
- Spacing between disparate thematic sections spans 80px to 112px on desktop, scaling down to 48px on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes surface differentiation, precise structural hairpins, and subtle tactile grounding rather than deep floating drops.

### Elevation Levels
- **Ground Floor (Flat / Recessed)**: `#F2F4F8` surfaces for secondary module sections, table stripes, and inner metric card wells. Elevation: none; structural separation via 1px `#E2E8F0` borders.
- **Level 1 (Card Baseline)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards mounted over `#F2F4F8` backdrops. Outlined with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, paired with an ambient institutional shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(21, 44, 61, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(21, 44, 61, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Hover Cards)**: Elevated interactive cards on hover. Border shifts to `rgba(29, 58, 80, 0.20)` with shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(21, 44, 61, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(21, 44, 61, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: Executive data briefs, filter menus, and compliance notices. Framed with a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on dark, `#CBD5E1` on light), cast with `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(21, 44, 61, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system relies on a **Soft** foundational geometry (`roundedness: 1`), keeping containers grounded, crisp, and executive, while selectively utilizing pill forms for focused micro-interactions.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Standard UI Cards & Containers**: `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radius provides a modern profile without sacrificing traditional institutional structure.
- **Input Fields, Dropdowns & Small Cards**: `0.25rem` (base radius) for crisp data entry and data sheets.
- **Pill Exceptions**:
  - Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary CTA buttons leverage full pill borders (`9999px`) to visually stand out against rectilinear analytical layouts.
  - Category tags, status pills, and stage markers use full pill borders (`9999px`).
- **Metric Circular Rings**: Full 50% circle displays used for analytical percentage representations and progress tracking.

## Components

### Header & Navigation
- **Global Header**: Background `#1D3A50` with pure white navigation typography (15px, 700 weight). Features a permanent, unyielding **3px Crimson (`#D11F4C`) horizontal rule** running along the complete lower edge.
- **Active Navigation Items**: Signaled with an active Crimson accent underline or text color, accompanied by subtle Gold (`#F8C945`) indicator dots for live states.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Crimson (`#D11F4C`) fill, pure white text, pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding: `12px 28px`. Hover state: Crimson Dark (`#B01840`) with an intentional translateY(-1px) elevation shift.
- **Secondary Navy CTA**: Deep Navy (`#1D3A50`) fill, pure white text, pill-shaped. Hover: `#152C3D`.
- **Outline / Editorial CTA**: Transparent background, 2px border in `#1D3A50` (or White `#FFFFFF` on dark bands), 15px bold text. Hover: Solid `#1D3A50` fill with White text.

### Section Labels & Hero Accents
- **Hero Accent Bar**: In the primary hero, the main headline is bounded on the left by an unbroken vertical Gold bar: `4px width`, solid `#F8C945`, spanning the full height of the headline group.
- **Section Headers**: Structured as an uppercase label (`01 / THE CLASSROOM NEED`) directly followed by a dominant h2 headline. 

### Metric Circular Displays & Timeline
- **Metric Rings**: Circular progress gauges using an SVG stroke model. Base ring: `#E2E8F0` (or `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)` on navy); active value ring: `#F8C945` or `#D11F4C`. Centered metrics display `metric-number` (44px, 900 weight).
- **Timeline Progression**: Connected via a continuous 2px vertical or horizontal line (`#E2E8F0`). Milestones display sequence numerals contained within circular Crimson (`#D11F4C`) badges with crisp white numbers.

### Data Tables
- **Table Headers**: Bold Navy typography on `#FFFFFF` or `#F2F4F8`. The entire `thead` row features a persistent **2px solid Academic Gold (`#F8C945`) bottom border**.
- **Rows**: Alternating zebra striping: Row A `#FFFFFF`, Row B `#F2F4F8`. Hairline horizontal dividers (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Cell padding: `14px 20px`. Text is 15px with tabular numerical alignment.

### Cards
- **Executive Card**: `#FFFFFF` background, `0.5rem` border radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, 24px to 32px interior padding.
- **Impact Card (Navy)**: Full `#1D3A50` fill, white typography, subtle gold header callout, 1px border in `rgba(248, 201, 69, 0.25)`.

### Form Controls
- **Inputs & Selects**: Height: 48px. White background with `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `0.25rem` radius. 16px body text. On focus: 1.5px border in `#1D3A50` with an outer ring of `rgba(29, 58, 80, 0.1)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px square/circle with a 1.5px border in `#1D3A50`. Checked state: Crimson (`#D11F4C`) fill with a white checkmark.

### Compliance & Regulatory Disclaimers
- Placed above the footer or beneath investment data in a muted slate container (`#F8FAFC`). Surfaced with a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` perimeter border, 12px caption typography (`#64748B`), and an explicit lock or regulatory icon in primary Navy.
