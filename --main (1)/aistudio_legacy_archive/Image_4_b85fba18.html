Art & Design

Drama & Roleplay

Character perspective taking, emotional regulation, narrative improvisation, and expressive vocal projection.

Key Pupil Outcomes:

Dance & Movement

Somatic awareness, rhythmic choreography, physical storytelling, and kinetic spatial balance.

Key Pupil Outcomes:

03 / PEDAGOGIC PROGRESSION

Structured Developmental Scaffolding (Early to Second Level)

Pedagogical progression mapped directly to Scottish Curriculum for Excellence levels, ensuring age-appropriate cognitive challenge, fine-motor calibration, and expressive fluency.

Stage Level Cohort Age Pedagogical Focus & Modality Objectives CfE Benchmark Metric
Early Level (P1) Ages 4–5 Sensory discovery & baseline motor control: Tactile confidence, imaginative play, free exploration of sound and texture, baseline gross/fine motor control, and associative color play. EXA 0-01a to EXA 0-14a: Explores media, sounds, and physical rhythms with confidence.
First Level (P2–P4) Ages 6–8 Collaborative storytelling & technique: Mixed-modality expression, foundational technique in drawing/sculpting, structured role-play improvisation, rhythmic synchrony, and peer appreciation. EXA 1-02a to EXA 1-15a: Creates and presents expressive works demonstrating intentional design.
Second Level (P5–P7) Ages 9–12 Critical creative thinking & autonomy: Independent portfolio projects, multi-disciplinary media production, stop-motion animation, nuanced characterization, and cultural contextual awareness. EXA 2-04a to EXA 2-18a: Evaluates personal creative output and integrates varied aesthetic styles.
